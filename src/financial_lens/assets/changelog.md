# What's new

## 0.9.3

- **Uploads page.** Every file you added, with the file itself next to everything that was read from it:
  amounts, dates, categories, tags, descriptions and line items. Change, add or delete records right there.
- **Delete an upload.** Deleting a receipt, invoice or statement also removes the records made from it.
  Upload it again later and it is read afresh.
- **No duplicates.** A receipt photographed twice, or a statement that overlaps one you already imported,
  no longer creates the same records again: the new file is attached to the records that exist.
- **Apertus 1.5 analyst** for computers with a strong graphics card (12 GB or more of video memory):
  Apertus reads every document as a whole (merchant, each item, VAT, total, category), and the program
  checks its arithmetic.
- **Version and What's new** in the app (sidebar and Settings) and in the Windows list of installed apps.

## 0.9.2

- Bank statement lines show the merchant instead of booking codes: Booking.com, Migros, SBB EasyRide,
  Starlink, "Cash withdrawal, BKB Vogesenplatz".
- Automatic categories: groceries, restaurants, transport, travel, cash, telecom, online shopping,
  subscriptions; the local AI suggests the rest if it is on.
- Transfers to your own account (e.g. Revolut in your name) count as own transfers, not as spending.
- Collective payment orders get their own category.
- Entries imported earlier are tidied once, unless you corrected them yourself.

## 0.9.1

- Windows: PostgreSQL no longer opens a console window that stopped the database when closed.
- Phones: the Phone page shows the address the phone connects to and, on Windows, a button to allow
  phones through the firewall.
- AI status is always visible at the top; the model switches on after downloading and comes back after
  a restart.
- "Read again" for documents added before the AI was on.

## 0.9.0

- Corporate edition: one server for a company, people with logins and roles, audit log, 3-month trial.
